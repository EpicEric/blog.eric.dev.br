# Product

## What this is

Epic Eric's personal technical blog: a static archive of notes on code, Nix, Rust, SSH, self-hosting, and whatever else is on the workbench. The voice is personal and technical, not a company publication.

## Audience

Developers and self-hosters who arrive from a link, a search, or the RSS feed and want to read a full technical entry without fighting the interface. Many read in Portuguese; the English and Portuguese archives are peers, not an afterthought.

## Jobs

- Scan the most recent posts and open one.
- Jump into a tag to see everything on that subject.
- Read long-form technical prose, tables, diagrams, and code comfortably on a phone or a desktop.
- Switch language without losing the current post or tag.
- Subscribe with RSS.
- Find Eric's main site, GitHub, Codeberg, Mastodon, and Ko-fi.

## Requirements

- US English at `/` and Brazilian Portuguese at `/pt-BR/`.
- Markdown content with fenced code, syntax highlighting, tables, images, captions, and headings.
- A post page with title, date, tags, and content.
- A page per tag in each locale.
- Per-locale RSS feeds.
- Light and dark themes, following the system on first visit and remembering an explicit choice.
- Responsive from 320px up; no horizontal page overflow.
- Static hosting only.
